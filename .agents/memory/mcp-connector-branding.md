---
name: MCP connector branding
description: FIA lion-head branding requirement and limits of client-owned connector rendering.
---

Use the FIA lion head to identify FIA Toolkit in connected AI software.

**Why:** The user wants the Academy's recognizable icon in their agentic tools,
not an unbranded connector.

**How to apply:** Advertise standard MCP branding with a public, same-origin
image and appropriate discovery metadata. Verify delivery separately from display:
third-party clients control their own UI and may ignore custom-connector icons.
Do not promise an icon appears in every client without testing that client's UI.
