Use a persistent database (PostgreSQL or SQLite) to store all check-in records. Each record must be tied to a user ID and a week identifier (ISO week number + year). No data is ever deleted or overwritten. New check-ins append to the history. The database schema should include:

user_id
week_number
year
date_submitted
videos_published (integer)
video_platforms (array or comma-separated -- YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
total_views (integer)
new_leads (integer)
email_list_size (integer)
revenue_this_week (decimal)
hours_worked (decimal)
weekly_win (text)
weekly_struggle (text)
next_week_goal (text)


WEEKLY CHECK-IN FORM

Number of videos published this week (number input)
Which platforms (multi-select checkboxes: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Other)
Total views across all platforms (number input)
New leads or opt-ins generated (number input)
Current email list size (number input)
Revenue generated this week in dollars (number input)
Hours worked on the business (number input)
One win to celebrate this week (text area)
