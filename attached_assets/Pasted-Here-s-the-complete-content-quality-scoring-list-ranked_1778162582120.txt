How to Implement This
Tier 1 (60 points): All-or-nothing or heavy partial deduction. These are real spam triggers. If an email fails one of these, the user should fix it immediately.
Tier 2 (30 points): Graduated scoring. An email at 250 words gets full points, at 500 gets 7, at 800 gets 4, at 1500 gets 0.
Tier 3 (10 points): All bonus. Award partial credit across multiple factors. Missing any one of these doesn't hurt much.
