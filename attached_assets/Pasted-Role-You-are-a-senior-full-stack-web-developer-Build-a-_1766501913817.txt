Scoring guidance
Each part is 0 to 20.
Provide consistent criteria:

0 to 5: missing

6 to 10: weak

11 to 15: solid

16 to 20: strong
Scoring must be grounded in what is actually present in the transcript. If something is missing, say it is missing.
